How to check a coupon

  1. Visit the official Temu app or website and sign in.
  2. Review coupon terms, account eligibility, minimum spend, and expiry.
  3. Apply the code only through the official promotion or checkout flow.
  4. Confirm the final price and discount in your order summary.

Egyptian pound offers

If a promotion displays an amount in Egyptian pounds (EGP), check the precise value and conditions shown by Temu. A stated amount such as EGP 3,000 should not be treated as a guaranteed discount unless the official offer confirms it for your account.

Before placing an order

  • Review shipping, delivery, returns, and refund conditions.
  • Check whether the offer applies to selected items only.
  • Protect your account credentials and never share one-time verification codes.
